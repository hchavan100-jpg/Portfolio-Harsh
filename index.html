(03) — About

Hi, I’m Harsh. I believe every logo deserves a little personality and every brand deserves a face you’d wave at on the street. I sketch first, vector later and overthink kerning always. When I’m not drawing, I’m collecting stickers, chasing street food, or gently explaining to people why their logo is lying to them.
